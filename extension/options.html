How automatic checks work
  • They start 1.5 seconds after you stop typing, at most six a minute, and only on sites you turned on.
  • Only the active paragraph (up to 2,000 characters) goes through Seatline to your provider. Model processing may be remote.
  • A check with no answer in 30 seconds is cancelled, and background checks then pause for five minutes.
  • In rich editors such as X, Accept changes the text through the editor, and its own undo reverses it. Gmail, LinkedIn, Slack, Notion and other editors Lineleaf has not verified show suggestions to copy.
  • Clearer wording adds one more request per paragraph and shows as dashed blue underlines. It never offers changes to names, numbers, dates or negations.
  • Live speed and quality are still being evaluated.
