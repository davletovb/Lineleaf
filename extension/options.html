Writing

The language Lineleaf checks, and how your selected provider answers your checks.

Provider and model apply to every check and rewrite after you save. Each provider remembers its saved preferences. Effort and speed are available for Codex only. Lower effort can answer faster; higher effort can help with difficult text. Use a native model ID supported by your installed provider runtime, or leave Model empty for its default. Claude Code and Grok require subscription sign-in. Gemini uses Antigravity with the explicit cloud opt-in above; Lineleaf stores no provider credentials.

Provider default uses your Codex speed setting. Standard requests normal processing. Fast requests faster processing and may use more subscription allowance, keeping your model and reasoning effort. Availability depends on your Codex version, model and plan. See current Codex speed and usage.

Türkçe offers corrections, rewrites and clearer wording, with Turkish explanations. Review rewrite warnings about negation, names and numbers. Lineleaf checks one language at a time, so switch here when you switch languages.

Automatic checks Experimental

Underline mistakes as you type, the way a proofreader would. Off until you turn it on.

  • The active paragraph (up to 2,000 characters) goes through Seatline to your selected provider. Model processing may be remote.
  • Checks wait 1.5 seconds after typing, with a shared ten-second interval and a cap of six attempts per minute.
  • A background check that gets no answer in 30 seconds (90 for something you ask for) is cancelled, and background checks then pause for five minutes.
  • In rich editors such as X, only the paragraph you are typing in is sent and suggestions are underlined. Accept changes the text only after you press it, through the editor’s own editing, and the editor’s undo reverses it.
  • Gmail, LinkedIn, Slack, Notion and editors Lineleaf has not verified show suggestions for you to copy; Lineleaf never edits those.
  • Live speed and quality are still being evaluated.
  • After each automatic correction check, Lineleaf sends the same paragraph once more and asks for wording that could be clearer or shorter.
  • These show as separate, dashed blue underlines labelled “Clearer wording · Optional style”, are never corrections, change nothing until you press Accept, and are never offered when they would change a name, number, date or negation.
  • It adds one more provider request per checked paragraph and shares the same ten-second interval and six-attempts-per-minute cap, so checks come more slowly.
  • Its precision is measured separately and has not been verified on a live provider.

Personal dictionary

Words Lineleaf should accept as correctly spelled.

Up to 500 words. Dictionary words are stored intentionally and sent with checks; spelling suggestions for these words are filtered.

    Privacy & stored data

    What Lineleaf keeps on this browser.

    • Lineleaf saves preferences, dictionary words, and enabled site origins.
    • Session storage keeps request-limit timestamps and the last manual check’s durations, requested provider and model, effort and speed, and outcome. Automatic and cancelled checks do not replace it. Drafts, answers and site addresses are excluded from timing details.
    • Suggestions from a check stay in that page's memory only, for as long as the text they describe is unchanged. They are dropped when you edit that text, leave or close the page, pause Lineleaf, or turn the site off, and are never saved.
    • The writing panel clears its selection and results after five minutes.
    • No draft history, provider credentials, or telemetry is stored.
