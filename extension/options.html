Writing preferences

Use a model supported by your installed Codex runtime. Sign in with your subscription; Lineleaf stores no provider credentials.

When enabled, the active paragraph (up to 2,000 characters) on sites you enable goes through Seatline to Codex. Model processing may be remote. Checks wait 1.5 seconds after typing, with a shared ten-second interval and six-attempts-per-minute cap. In rich editors such as X, automatic checking sends only the paragraph you are typing in and underlines suggestions; Accept changes the text only after you press it, through the editor’s own editing, and the editor’s undo reverses it. Gmail, LinkedIn, Slack, Notion and editors Lineleaf has not verified show suggestions for you to copy; Lineleaf never edits those. Live speed and quality are still being evaluated.

Up to 500 words. Dictionary words are stored intentionally and sent with checks; spelling suggestions for these words are filtered.

Enabled sites

Enable a site from the extension popup. Disabling it cancels its requests and removes its access when no other enabled origin needs that permission.

    Stored data

    Lineleaf saves preferences, dictionary words, and enabled site origins. Only automatic-request and provider-backoff timestamps are kept in session storage to preserve the shared cap across worker restarts. Check field snapshots expire after five minutes or when focus/site state changes. No draft history, provider credentials, or telemetry is stored.
